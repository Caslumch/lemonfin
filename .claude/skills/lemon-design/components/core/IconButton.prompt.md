Round, icon-only button. `grape` is the signature round "+", `dark-ghost` sits on black cards, `light` is the white bell on dark frames.

```jsx
<IconButton variant="grape" icon={<PlusIcon />} ariaLabel="Adicionar" />
<IconButton variant="light" size="lg" icon={<BellIcon />} ariaLabel="Notificações" />
```

Variants: `grape | lemon | dark | light | ghost | dark-ghost`. Sizes `sm | md | lg`. `rounded="md"` for a squircle instead of a circle.
